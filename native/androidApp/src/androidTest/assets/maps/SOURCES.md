# Fixture

`box.glb` is the Khronos glTF Sample Assets Box model, used only in tests.

[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).

Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Box

It is not part of the application or the commercial map model catalogue.

SHA-256 of `box.glb`:
`ed52f7192b8311d700ac0ce80644e3852cd01537e4d62241b9acba023da3d54e`.

`box-meshopt.glb` is the same attributed geometry, modified by encoding with
`@gltf-transform/core/extensions/functions` 4.5.1 and `meshoptimizer` 1.3.0,
using `meshopt({ encoder: MeshoptEncoder, level: 'medium' })`.
It exercises `EXT_meshopt_compression` and `KHR_mesh_quantization` in Filament.
The committed fixtures make normal Android checks independent of Node/model tools.

SHA-256 of `box-meshopt.glb`:
`582fb8aabc88acdfae6e99323ccf66240a793b393b5682d57c900bc647f8ee68`.

`checker-basis-lz.ktx2` and `checker-uastc.ktx2` are generated two-colour
32×32 test textures, encoded with Khronos toktx 4.4.0, mipmaps and sRGB.
The UASTC fixture uses Zstandard level 18. They contain no third-party image.
`box-water.glb` adds UV coordinates, that UASTC texture and a low-roughness PBR material to
the attributed, meshopt-compressed Box above. All three are test assets only.

- `checker-basis-lz.ktx2`: `27769556c5167a51d75a7dca6c7ba6adde27d9a7bc9ff70ef1fd8f9e7d6004ed`.
- `checker-uastc.ktx2`: `b233a6cfa34725197c0cfdd95feabb5fddd348d637e9eb1f3023bdcd93bd09f5`.
- `box-water.glb`: `88750f035c515c7cd8347d737e2c2c7964d83d8a7211eaca86ea3da6c477d93a`.

`box-shadow-floor.glb` — производное того же CC-BY Box: добавлен родительский
узел scale=[8,0.02,8] для освещаемой поверхности в проверке точечных теней.
Геометрия, материалы и исходная атрибуция сохранены.
SHA-256: `2734c080e514239cf208a4c5f39e35246e05e5192d3a4bf9cf1c98c5dfb89de2`.

`box-lod-green.glb` — тот же CC-BY Box, baseColorFactor заменён на [0,0.8,0,1]
для различения LOD и render в проверке фоновой смены качества.
SHA-256: `6b2f7d8b99aa336ba7d8f678b2d7d0a7c3d82cc151faa10bb91a7dd720239268`.

`box-pbr-basis-lz.glb` and `box-pbr-uastc.glb` keep that attributed meshopt
Box geometry and generated checker textures. They use ordinary gltfio PBR,
without native water behaviour. Rebuild with
`python3 scripts/native/generate-map-texture-fixtures.py`.

- `box-pbr-basis-lz.glb`: `b8527ade48677b2f3226df2d5200663ed2c0f54784881bf9ac83c3fe9efe2ea2`.
- `box-pbr-uastc.glb`: `d741488842b4cd7e372d45a964a6fca989496ff646d528ca77c48eb97711410f`.

`box-water-custom.glb` adds the catalogue-style normalized unsigned-byte VEC2
`_WATER` attribute to `box-water.glb` without changing its geometry or textures.
Generated by `scripts/native/generate-map-custom-attribute-fixture.py` and shared
with JVM fixtures. It verifies that gltfio receives only supported semantics,
while native water keeps the original coverage/flow data.
SHA-256: `d8858535b6105bd712d71da5a1ea99ab6975e4dda2e4836b64915505ea1c337e`.
