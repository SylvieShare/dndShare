Both hind feet firmly planted and clearly separated. Wings partially raised and folded back in a compact symmetrical V, thick connected leathery membranes and sturdy finger bones, wing tips clear of ground. Tail curves beside the body above ground so full hooked stinger is visible. Readable ochre-brown scales, paler tan wing membranes, matte hand-painted miniature finish. Elevated three-quarter front side view with whole wings, both feet, tail and stinger visible with margin. Soft neutral studio light, robust sculptural volumes.
